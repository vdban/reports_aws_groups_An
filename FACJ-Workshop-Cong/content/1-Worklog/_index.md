**On this page**, I summarize my internship worklog for the **SmartHome_IoT** project at FCAJ. The internship lasts **8 weeks**, from **08/06/2026** to **31/07/2026**. I serve as **Team Lead**, responsible for **system architecture**, **database design**, **module integration** (backend–frontend–IoT), and **Hugo workshop documentation**. The team includes An (UI), Công (backend), Phúc (IoT/firmware), and Quỳnh (DevOps).

Over 8 weeks, I learned core AWS services (account, Budgets, CLI, IAM, VPC, EC2, IAM Roles, S3, RDS, CloudWatch, DynamoDB, Lambda, API Gateway, Cognito) and delivered the project in parallel from week 6 — finalizing architecture, DB schema, end-to-end integration, and the internship report.
