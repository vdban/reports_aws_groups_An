# Event Report: Agenic AI Buildwick 2026

## Purpose of the Event

- Provide participants with a hands-on opportunity to transform ideas into working AI-powered solutions within a limited time and present them to a panel of judges.
- Encourage participants to rethink traditional workflows by leveraging Artificial Intelligence and modern cloud technologies to solve real-world business challenges.
- Create an environment for networking, collaboration, and teamwork among participants from both technical and business backgrounds.

---

## Speakers

- **Nguyen Gia Hung** – Head of Solution Architecture, Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam
- **Joseph Marazota** – Head of Technology, ASEAN
- Representatives from the participating Hackathon teams:
  - One Team
  - Signal Scale
  - Plan
  - 3K
  - Six Pillar

---

## Key Highlights

### 1. AI Chatbot for Food Ordering

The **One Team** developed an AI-powered chatbot for KFC that integrates directly with Zalo and WhatsApp.

Key features:

- Customers can order food without leaving their messaging application.
- No additional application installation is required.
- Simplifies the ordering process.
- Improves customer experience and conversion rates.

---

### 2. Multi-Agent System for Business Strategy

The **Signal Scale** team built a multi-agent AI platform that assists business strategy teams.

The solution can:

- Collect financial reports automatically.
- Analyze competitors' information.
- Simulate Return on Investment (ROI) for different business strategies.
- Support faster and more informed decision-making.

---

### 3. AI Solution Architect Assistant

The **Plan** team developed an AI assistant designed for AWS Solution Architects.

Its capabilities include:

- Understanding natural language requirements.
- Automatically generating AWS architecture diagrams.
- Estimating infrastructure costs.
- Producing Terraform Infrastructure-as-Code templates.

---

### 4. Crowd Monitoring System

The **3K** team introduced **Sheper**, a real-time crowd monitoring platform powered by:

- AWS Kinesis
- YOLO Computer Vision

The system is capable of:

- Processing live video streams.
- Tracking crowd density.
- Predicting congestion in airports, shopping malls, and public events.

---

### 5. AI-Powered Anti-Money Laundering

The **Six Pillar** team presented an **Adaptive Workflow Engine** for Anti-Money Laundering (AML).

Multiple AI Agents collaborate to:

- Investigate suspicious financial transactions.
- Automate AML workflows.
- Reduce false-positive alerts.
- Minimize manual investigation time.

## What I Learned

### Design Thinking

One of the most valuable lessons was to always begin with the **business problem**, rather than focusing solely on technology.

An AI solution only creates value when it addresses real customer or business needs.

The event also emphasized the importance of building a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** before expanding additional features.

---

### Technical Architecture

The Hackathon demonstrated how multiple AWS services can be combined to build intelligent AI applications, including:

- Amazon Bedrock
- AWS Lambda
- Amazon DynamoDB
- AWS Fargate

I also learned techniques for reducing AI hallucinations by using:

- Amazon Bedrock Guardrails
- Human-in-the-loop validation
- Cross-checking results with additional AI Agents

---

### Teamwork and Project Management

The event highlighted the importance of effective project management.

Key lessons include:

- Proper scope management is essential during short development cycles.
- Team members should align around a common objective.
- Communication and conflict resolution are just as important as technical skills.
